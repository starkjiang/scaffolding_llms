# Row Crop Agronomy Quick Reference (training data, fictional)

## Corn Growth Stages
- VE: emergence. V3: three leaf collars visible, roughly 2–3 weeks after emergence.
- V6: growing point moves above the soil surface; frost damage risk changes.
- VT: tasseling. R1: silking — the most sensitive stage for drought stress.
- R6: physiological maturity (black layer).

## Soybean Growth Stages
- VC: unifoliate leaves unrolled. V3: third trifoliate.
- R1: beginning bloom. R3: beginning pod. R5: beginning seed.
- Herbicide applications on soybean are typically restricted after R1; check the product label.

## Nitrogen
Typical corn nitrogen application in the Corn Belt ranges from 150 to 200 lb N/acre depending on yield goal, soil organic matter, and previous crop. Soybean fixes its own nitrogen and normally receives none.

## Scouting Thresholds
- Soybean aphid: treat at 250 aphids per plant with populations increasing, through R5.
- Corn rootworm beetles: 0.75 beetles per plant in July signals risk for next year's corn.
