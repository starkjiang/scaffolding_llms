# HX-900 Combine — Operator's Manual Excerpt (training data, fictional)

## Section 4: Diagnostic Codes
### E212 — Hydraulic pressure low
Cause: Hydraulic fluid below minimum, clogged suction filter, or worn pump.
Steps:
1. Park on level ground, engine off, check reservoir sight glass. Fill to the upper mark with HX Hydraulic Fluid 46.
2. Inspect suction filter (part HX-F-4410). Replace if discolored or clogged.
3. If code persists after 1 and 2, contact dealer service: pump inspection required.

### E305 — Feeder house chain speed sensor fault
Cause: Sensor gap out of range or wiring damage.
Steps:
1. Check sensor gap: 1.5–2.5 mm. Adjust using the two M8 bolts.
2. Inspect harness for chafing near the feeder house pivot.
3. Replace sensor (part HX-S-2270) if fault persists.

### E410 — Engine coolant temperature high
Cause: Blocked radiator screen, low coolant, fan clutch fault.
Steps:
1. Stop operation immediately. Allow engine to idle for 3 minutes before shutdown.
2. Clean rotating radiator screen. Check coolant level when cool.
3. Do not resume operation until temperature returns to normal. If it recurs, contact dealer service.

## Section 7: Maintenance Intervals
- Engine oil and filter: every 250 hours.
- Hydraulic filter HX-F-4410: every 500 hours or at E212.
- Feeder house chain tension: every 50 hours.
