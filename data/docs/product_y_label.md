# Product Y Fungicide — Specimen Label (training data, fictional)

## Crops and Application Rates
| Crop | Rate | Max applications per season | Pre-harvest interval (PHI) |
|------|------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| Corn | 6.0 fl oz/acre | 2 | 14 days |
| Soybean | 4.0 fl oz/acre | 2 | 21 days |

## Restrictions
- Re-entry interval (REI): 4 hours.
- Apply at first sign of disease; a second application may be made 14 days later.
- Do not apply more than 12 fl oz/acre per season on corn.

## Resistance Management
Rotate with fungicides from a different FRAC group. Do not make more than two consecutive applications.
