# Approval Thresholds (training data, fictional)

## Who approves what
| Amount | Approver | Typical turnaround |
|--------|----------|--------------------|
| Under $100 | Automatic, no approval needed | immediate |
| $100 – $999 | Your direct manager | 2 working days |
| $1,000 – $9,999 | Department head | 5 working days |
| $10,000 and above | Finance director and department head | 10 working days |

## Always needs a human, whatever the amount
- Anything involving a client, a supplier, or a government official.
- Anything where the receipt is missing or illegible.
- Anything submitted more than 60 days after the expense date.
- Any claim that has already been rejected once.

## Splitting
Deliberately splitting one purchase into several smaller claims to stay under a
threshold is a policy violation and is reported to Finance, regardless of intent.
