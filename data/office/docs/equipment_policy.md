# Equipment and Home Office Policy (training data, fictional)

## Standard issue
Every employee receives a laptop, one external monitor, a keyboard, and a mouse.
Replacement cycle is three years, or sooner if the device fails.

## Home office allowance
- $500 once every two years, for a desk, chair, lighting, or similar.
- A second monitor may be claimed from this allowance.
- Furniture over $500 requires a written ergonomic assessment first.

## Phones
- A monthly stipend of $45 is available to employees whose role requires a
  phone for work. It is not available by default — your manager nominates you.
- The company does not reimburse handset purchases.

## Software and subscriptions
- Anything under $100 per year: approve it with your manager.
- $100 or more per year: it must go through IT review before purchase, because
  of data-handling and licence-compliance requirements.
- Personal subscriptions used for work are not reimbursable.

## Equipment when leaving
All company equipment is returned on the last working day. The home office
allowance does not need to be repaid.
